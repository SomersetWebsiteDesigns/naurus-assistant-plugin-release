# Naurus

Your business, in your own assistant.

Connect an existing Naurus account, choose your business and approve the listed permissions. Your assistant account can use a different email address. A Naurus account and an eligible assistant account are required.

## Start here

After adding this plugin through your assistant’s official directory, ask: **“Which Naurus business am I connected to?”**

Naurus shows the selected business, permissions and connection status under Connections. A successful sign-in is not proof that a workflow has run.

## Included workflows

- **Prepare a social post:** save wording you approve as a private draft for a connected page. It does not schedule or publish.
- **Open in Naurus:** create a private upload handoff for a static HTML ZIP. Upload it in Naurus, review the conversion and edit the project. Publishing remains a separate human decision. Supports up to 20 MiB compressed, 200 files and 20 pages; it is not a pixel-identical CSS, JavaScript or backend importer.
- **Domain guidance:** get official Cloudflare setup guidance for an authorised website. This changes no DNS records.

Both assistants use your existing Naurus subscription and shared allowance. Supplied draft text is not regenerated or charged as new Naurus inference. Your assistant’s subscription limits are separate.

## Permissions and data

Only the Naurus user and business you authorise are accessible. These workflows cannot publish, send messages or access files on your computer. Disconnect one assistant under Naurus → Connections without disconnecting another. This stops future Naurus access; it does not erase information already shared with your assistant or remove its installed plugin.

Domain guidance sends a fixed public topic to an approved documentation service, not customer content or your Naurus token. Hosted ChatGPT and Claude connections cannot quote, buy or reconcile paid supplier inspections, even if an older grant retained a paid scope. They do not transfer funds or offer checkout. Paid supplier inspections through Nauri or an explicitly approved native client use separate configuration, human task approval and spending limits. The standalone paid import service is also separate from this hosted account integration.

Claude Code’s file uploader is a separate installation and approval. It is not included in this hosted-account bundle.
